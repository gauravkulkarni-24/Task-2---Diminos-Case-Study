# Diminos Store – Delivery Time Analysis (Case Study)

## 📌 Problem Overview
Kanav has started his own pizza store by taking a franchise from **Diminos**.  
Diminos promises pizza delivery within **31 minutes**, failing which the order is free.

Recently, Diminos introduced a strict performance metric:
> **95th percentile of delivery time must be less than 31 minutes**

Kanav is concerned that failing this metric could result in losing the franchise.  
This case study analyzes the store’s delivery performance to help Kanav understand risks and take corrective actions.

---

## 🎯 Objective
As a freelance data scientist, the goal of this analysis is to:
- Evaluate delivery time performance
- Check compliance with the 95th percentile rule
- Identify patterns, delays, and risks
- Provide insights to help improve operational efficiency

---

## 📂 Dataset
- **File Name:** `diminos_data.csv`
- **Description:** Contains order delivery time data for the Diminos store

## 🧪 Analysis Performed
- Data understanding and cleaning  
- Delivery time distribution analysis  
- Percentile (95th percentile) calculation  
- Identification of delayed deliveries  
- Performance evaluation against the 31-minute benchmark  
- Insight generation for business decision-making  

---

## 📊 Key Insights
- The delivery time distribution highlights peak delay periods
- The 95th percentile metric helps identify worst-case delivery scenarios
- Certain orders exceed the promised delivery threshold, posing a business risk
- Operational improvements are required to consistently meet the franchise criteria
